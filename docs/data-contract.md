# Canonical Data Contract: Chennai Traffic Intelligence Platform

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** Approved Specification (Review 2 Baseline)

---

## 1. Overview & Data Tiering

All data flows strictly adhere to 4 operational state tiers:
- `OBSERVED`: Ground-truth values collected from sensors/logs.
- `DERIVED`: Computed metrics based on explicit mathematical formulas.
- `PREDICTED`: Values generated by external ML inference models.
- `SIMULATED`: Synthetic development data (strictly watermarked in UI).

## 3. Mathematical Formulas for Derived Metrics & Priority Methodology

### A. Traffic Utilization ($U$)
$$U = \frac{\text{vehicle\_count}}{\text{road\_capacity}}$$

### B. Speed Reduction / Deficit ($R_v$)
$$R_v = \max\left(0.0, \, \frac{\text{speed\_limit} - \text{average\_speed}}{\text{speed\_limit}}\right)$$

### C. Congestion Index ($CI$)
A standardized composite index normalized to $[0, 100]$:
$$CI = \min\left(100.0, \, \left(0.45 \cdot \min\left(1.0, \frac{U}{1.2}\right) \cdot 100 + 0.55 \cdot R_v \cdot 100\right)\right)$$

### D. Decision Support Priority Score Methodology ($PS$)
The Priority Score is designed exclusively as an operational **Decision Support** indicator for traffic dispatchers and authorities to triage congested corridors and incident hotspots. It does NOT perform autonomous signal control.

#### Scoring Formula:
$$PS = \min\left(100.0, \, \left(0.40 \cdot CI + 0.30 \cdot (R_v \cdot 100) + 0.20 \cdot \min(100, \text{accident\_count} \times 50) + 0.10 \cdot (\text{is\_peak\_hour} ? 100 : 0)\right)\right)$$

#### Weighting Justification:
1. **40% Congestion Index ($CI$):** Primary composite baseline reflecting volume strain and slowdown.
2. **30% Normalized Speed Deficit ($R_v$):** Direct metric of vehicular delay below municipal design limit.
3. **20% Accident Multiplier:** Immediate safety escalation (each active incident adds 10 points, capped at 20 points).
4. **10% Peak Hour Flag:** Tactical priority boost during designated Chennai morning/evening commute windows.

#### Priority Tiers:
- **`Critical`** ($PS \ge 75$ OR $CI \ge 75 \land \text{accident\_count} \ge 1$): Immediate dispatch recommended.
- **`High`** ($55 \le PS < 75$): Heavy congestion requiring cycle time adjustments.
- **`Medium`** ($35 \le PS < 55$): Moderate volume, monitoring recommended.
- **`Low`** ($PS < 35$): Normal free-flow conditions.

## 4. Null & Missing Data Policy

- Missing **`weather_condition`** $\rightarrow$ Display `"Weather Data Unavailable"`.
- Missing **`accident_count`** $\rightarrow$ Default to `0`, UI indicates `"No Active Incidents"`.
- Missing **`road_capacity`** $\rightarrow$ Default to `3600 veh/hr`.
- Missing **`speed_limit`** $\rightarrow$ Default to `50.0 km/h`.
